Student Tech Fan Club Webring

Are you a Student-Run Tech club? Are you interested in joining the STFC webring? Well, you've come to the right place!

To Join the Webring...

To join the ring, sinply E-mail cif_board@u.rochester.edu, expressing your desire to join, as well as a little bit about your club, and its website. We're students too, so we like to hear all about people's nerdy projects! We should E-Mail you back within a few days, once you've been added to the directory.

Once that's all been done, and you've been added to the directory, go on to step 2

Linking to the Webring on your site

Once you've been added to the webring, you should add some for of reference to STFC, in the form of links. At minimum, we'd recommend the "next" and "previous" links, as well as the "STFC image" linking to the directory on this site (https://stfc.cif.rochester.edu.

This is cool! But, why?

We thought it was a pretty nifty idea. We liked looking at other Student-run Technical organizations at various other institutions, and thought it would be a cool, nifty little thing to have a single page for use to do that with!

The idea of a webring is quite old, coming from ye olde web, but it is a good idea, since we're all related by a shared love of technology, and this provides a way for us all to meet each other, to share resources, and to advance our goals to learn more about technology.

If you are a student-run technical organization, feel free to reach out! We'd love to see your stuff! : )
